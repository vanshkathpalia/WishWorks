problem — a champagne bottle foil is sold at both 12-14" and 32"; a star foil at 12" and 18";
latex at 5", 10" and 12". So decide the size in this order, and put which rule you used in the
source column:

2. SIZE WORDS ARE SIZES. small / mini / big / large / jumbo / XL in my line mean the smaller or

3. ONE STANDARD ONLY. If the item genuinely has a single retail size, fill it in.
   Source: "standard".

5. NO STANDARD AT ALL (sash, glue dots, tape, ribbon) — "not commonly stated". That card gets no

STEP 2 — after I confirm, generate the image.

Layout
- 1:1 square, highest resolution you can produce. Pure white background.
- A grid of rounded-corner cards, thin light-pink border on each, generous white gutters,

- Header, centred at the top: "ITEMS INCLUDED" in very large heavy sans-serif, alternating

- Under it, one solid-colour pill strip in the accent colour, white text:
  "PERFECT DECORATION FOR <OCCASION>".
- Footer pill, bottom centre: "TOTAL CONTENTS: <N> PIECES", and below it in small grey type:

Every card

  Omit the dimension line entirely for anything that came back "not commonly stated".
