# New listing, one button — the guide

This is the whole job of putting a new kit on Flipkart, in the app's **New** tab. You do two things
yourself: **check the pictures**, and **read the finished draft before Send to QC**. The app does the rest.

> **The lit button is always the next thing to press.** It has a pulsing orange outline. If you are ever
> unsure what to do, click any empty space on the screen and the app scrolls to it.

## Before you start (once per kit)

1. **Cost the kit** in *Costing → Cost a kit*: drop the inventory photo, send the costing chat, check
   every line's match and colour, press **Keep**. The counts you keep here are the counts every picture
   and every listing field will use, so this is the one screen where a mistake spreads everywhere.
2. **Put the inventory photo in the kit's folder** in Downloads, named `2.png` (or `2.jpg`), for example
   `Downloads/Whatsapp DW/ANP/ANP 4/2.png`. The New tab says *no inventory photo* until it finds one.
3. **Chrome must be logged in to Flipkart, and ChatGPT to its account**, in the app's own windows.

## The four steps

| # | Step | Who | What happens |
|---|------|-----|--------------|
| 1 | **Make images** | automatic | Tick up to 10 kits, press **Make images for the picked kits**. ChatGPT gets the kit's exact counts from our software, must repeat them back (*READY — 9 lines, 66 displayed, 69 total*), then draws the main photo and the "what's in the box" picture, and asks about sizes for the third. One kit at a time; each takes about 4 minutes. Leave ChatGPT and Chrome alone while it runs. |
| 2 | **Your check** | **you** | Each picture appears side by side. Count the balloons, foils and cutouts against the kit. **Anything wrong?** Type what is wrong under that picture (*"6 hearts, kit has 8"*) and press **Redo**. Only that picture is redrawn, in the same chat. The sizes question: type the sizes you actually pack and press **Send sizes**, or **Leave out** picture 3; it is optional. |
| 3 | **To Flipkart** | automatic, one press | When the pictures are right, press **Images are right — continue to Flipkart**. The app writes the listing text, files it, puts the parcel size on it, names the pictures, opens a **new Flipkart draft** under your trade name, uploads the pictures and fills every tab. About 6 minutes. |
| 4 | **Your read** | **you** | Press **Open the draft in Chrome**. Read every tab. Fix anything the app marked for your eyes. **Then press Send to QC in Chrome yourself.** The app never sends to QC. |

## When something stops

The kit's card turns red and says what stopped it, in plain words. Press **Try again**: it starts from
the step that stopped, not from the beginning. *What happened* under each card lists every step with
its time, if you need to tell Vansh.

| It says | Do this |
|---------|---------|
| *No inventory photo* | Put `2.png` in the kit's folder (above). |
| *No kit saved* | Cost the kit first. |
| *ChatGPT read the kit as … — ours is …* | The READY check caught a misreading before any picture was made. Press **Try again**. |
| *Flipkart is logged out* | Log in once in the app's Chrome, then **Try again**. |
| *Image upload: …* | Flipkart refused a picture; the reason is in its own words. Redo that picture, then **Try again**. |

## Things to know

- **Pictures are never perfectly counted by the AI.** Balloon counts in the dozens are the hardest; the
  main photo often shows more balloons than the kit has. That is exactly why step 2 is yours. The
  "what's in the box" picture is usually right, because its numbers are printed labels.
- The ChatGPT chats are named after the kit: `ANP004 — images`, `ANP004 — meta`. Find them in the
  ChatGPT sidebar by the SKU.
- Settings → **Flipkart trade name** is the brand on every new draft (PartyDreams unless changed).
- Kits already live on Flipkart are hidden; tick *show kits already live* to see them.
