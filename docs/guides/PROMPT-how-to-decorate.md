=== WORK THE WHOLE PICTURE OUT FROM THE PACK, NOT FROM ANY EXAMPLE ===

Everything below is decided by reading the PACK above. There is no default kit, no default
occasion and no default colour scheme. If two listings have different contents, the two pictures
must come out different.

1. THE OCCASION. If I filled in OCCASION, use exactly that. If I left it blank, read it off the
   pack — a printed banner, a foil phrase, a themed prop or a character usually names it outright.
   If the pack genuinely does not say, ask me in one line and stop. Do not guess, and do not carry
   over an occasion from anything you have seen before this message.
2. THE COLOURS. Two accent colours, taken from the balloons and foils in the pack itself. If the
   pack is blue and silver, the picture is blue and silver. Never use a colour that is not in the
   kit.
3. THE STEPS. Read the accessories in the pack and write the real assembly order for THIS kit —
   between three and five steps. A step may only use an item that is in the pack:
   - an inflating step names the pump ONLY if a pump is in the pack; otherwise it does not
     mention one;
   - an arch or garland step names the arch tape ONLY if arch tape is in the pack; without it,
     say how the balloons are actually tied or taped up instead;
   - a fixing step names the glue dots or glue tape ONLY if they are in the pack;
   - the last step hangs and fixes the named foils, banners, curtains and props that ARE in the
     pack, and ends with the decoration finished and ready to photograph.
   An instruction telling a buyer to use something that is not in the box is the worst mistake
   this picture can make: it reads as a missing item and comes back as a return.
4. THE COUNTS. Every number on the picture comes from the pack, exactly as written there.

=== LAYOUT ===

- 1:1 square, highest resolution you can produce. Pure white background. Nothing overlapping,
  everything upright, generous white space.

- HEADER, centred at the top: "HOW TO DECORATE" in very large heavy sans-serif, in the darker of
  the two accent colours, with a small heart or star flourish either side.
- Under it, a solid pill strip in the accent colour with white text: "IN <N> EASY STEPS", where
  <N> is the number of steps you actually drew, written as a word or a numeral consistently. A
  thin rule runs left and right of the pill.

- THE STEPS ROW — one rounded-corner card per step, side by side, equal width, thin light border:
  - a small numbered circle at the top of each card, in the accent colour, white numeral;
  - the step title in bold capitals, two lines maximum;
  - two or three short sentences underneath in plain dark blue-grey text, telling the buyer what
    to do in the order they will do it. Say it the way a person explains a job, not the way a
    manual does;
  - a photorealistic picture UNDER the text showing that step actually happening, built only from
    items in the pack. A hand doing the work is welcome. The last card shows the finished
    decoration, and it must look like the same kit the other cards were building;
  - a small arrow between consecutive cards, pointing to the next one.

- "PACKAGE INCLUDES" — a solid pill strip in the accent colour, white capitals, centred, with a
  small flourish either side, then a grid of the pack contents underneath, thin dividing lines
  between them:
  - one cell per line of the pack, in the pack's own order;
  - a photorealistic cut-out of ONE SINGLE PIECE — one balloon, one foil, one roll. Never a bunch,
    a pile or a fan of them. The count is written, not drawn;
  - under the picture, the count in bold accent colour exactly as "20 pcs" / "1 pc", and under
    that the item name in dark grey on up to two lines;
  - an item made of separate parts (a foil letter banner, bunting, a letter or number set) is
    drawn assembled here, small enough to sit in its cell, because that is what the buyer
    recognises it by.

- FOOTER STRIP, full width, in the accent colour with white text: three or four short claims with
  a simple line icon each. Every one must be a plain fact about THIS kit, and must be true of it:
  what it is for, that it goes up without tools, that it is a complete set, what occasions it
  genuinely suits. Read them back against the pack before you draw them.

=== HARD RULES ===

- NO QUALITY CLAIMS ANYWHERE ON THE IMAGE. Never "premium", "luxury", "royal", "best",
  "high quality", "100% original", "superior", "finest", "exclusive". These are unverifiable and
  the marketplace has flagged that kind of wording on our listings before. Say what the thing IS
  or what it DOES: "complete kit with pump and tape", "easy to assemble", "reusable", "ready for
  photos". A claim a buyer could check is worth more than one they cannot.
- No brand name, no logo, no watermark, no price, no discount, no offer badge, no urgency wording
  ("limited stock", "trending", "best seller"), no phone number, email, website or social handle.
  Any of these can get the catalogue rejected outright.
- If IMAGE 2 carries a seller tag or code anywhere (Meesho stamps one near the bottom-left), do
  not reproduce it. The rebuilt background must be completely clean.
- Every count on the picture matches the pack exactly. Read all of them back before generating.
- Spell every word correctly and read the text in the rendered image; a doubled or dropped letter
  in a heading kills the listing.

=== BEFORE YOU FINISH, CHECK ===

- Does every step use only items that are in the pack? Name each item you drew or mentioned and
  find it in the pack. If one is not there, rewrite that step.
- Does the pill say the same number of steps as the number of cards you drew?
- Does every "PACKAGE INCLUDES" cell show ONE piece, with its count in writing above the name?
- Is every count identical to the pack, and is the item order the same?
- Are the two accent colours colours that are actually in this kit?
- Is there any quality claim, price, badge, brand or seller tag anywhere on the image? Remove it.
- Would this picture be wrong for a different kit? It should be — if it would suit any kit
  equally, you have drawn a generic template instead of reading the pack.
