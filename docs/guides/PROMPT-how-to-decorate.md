=== ONE SECTION. FIVE STEPS. ===

The whole square is a single five-step guide:

  header  ->  ONE ROW OF FIVE TALL STEP CARDS  ->  one thin footer strip

Nothing else goes in the image. No "PACKAGE INCLUDES", no "ITEMS INCLUDED", no product grid,
no second row of product photos with piece counts under them.

That grid is the specific mistake to avoid. The last version of this prompt produced four short
cards on top and a nine-product grid underneath, which reads as two infographics stacked. The
other listing images already show what is in the box; this one exists to show that the buyer can
put it up himself, so the five cards get the whole square.

=== EVERYTHING COMES OUT OF THE PACK ===

There is no default kit, occasion, colour scheme or assembly method. Two different packs must
produce two different images.

OCCASION — use the one written above if it is filled in. If it is blank, read it off the pack:
printed banners, foil wording, themed foils, characters, occasion-specific props. If the pack
genuinely does not say, ask me in one line and STOP — do not guess, and do not carry an occasion
over from an earlier image or conversation.

COLOURS — two accents, both taken from the products themselves (black and gold for a groom kit,
blue and silver for a baby one). Never an accent colour that is not visibly in the kit. Use them
for the header, the numbered circles, the arrows, the card borders and the footer strip. The
background stays pure white.

THE FIVE STEPS — exactly five, describing the real assembly of THIS pack. Not three, not four,
not six. A simple kit gets its installation split into five useful stages, not fewer cards.

This is a planning framework, not a set of step names to copy:

  1  prepare / inflate the latex balloons
  2  build the balloon garland or arrangement
  3  put up the backdrop or curtain
  4  inflate and hang the foil balloons and wording pieces
  5  arrange everything and show the finished decoration

Adjust every one of them to the pack. No backdrop in the pack means no backdrop step — use the
next genuine stage instead. And the rule that matters most:

  NEVER SHOW OR MENTION A TOOL OR ACCESSORY THAT IS NOT IN THE PACK.

Arch tape, glue dots, glue tape, a pump, hooks, ribbon, a straw, a stand — each one appears only
if the pack lists it. If balloons are joined without an included arch tape, describe the tying
method instead, so nothing implies the tape is in the box. Ordinary hands, tying and hanging are
always fine. Check every object drawn in every card against the pack before you generate.

=== WHAT EACH CARD SAYS ===

  - a numbered circle, 1 to 5
  - a bold title of one or two short words per line ("INFLATE BALLOONS", "SETUP BACKDROP")
  - TWO short instruction sentences, as two small separate paragraphs
  - one photorealistic photo of that action being done, using only products from the pack

Card 1 shows a few balloons being inflated close up — a few, never the whole quantity.
Card 5 shows the COMPLETE FINISHED DECORATION, and it must obviously be the same kit that was
assembled in cards 1 to 4: same balloon colours, same foil shapes, same wording, same backdrop,
same sash. Nothing decorative in it that the pack does not contain. It is the strongest visual
in the image.

Each card's photo is different and shows progress toward that final one. A hand may appear where
it helps. Never reuse one photo twice, never a generic stock party scene.

COUNTS — every number written anywhere comes from the pack, never from IMAGE 2, never from an
earlier image, never estimated. A card may show a handful of balloons as a demonstration; it must
not look like a count of the 20 the pack lists. The picture demonstrates the action, the pack
holds the quantities.

=== LAYOUT ===

1:1 square, highest resolution available, pure white background.

HEADER, centred at the top:
  "HOW TO DECORATE" in very large heavy sans-serif capitals — "HOW TO" in near-black, "DECORATE"
  in the accent colour — with a small solid star on each side.
  Directly under it, a solid rounded pill in the accent colour with white capitals reading
  "IN 5 EASY STEPS", a thin accent rule running from each end of the pill to the edge.
  The number is 5. Not 3, not 4.

THE FIVE CARDS: one horizontal row of five TALL, narrow, equal-width cards spanning the full
width — never three on one row and two on another, which is what makes it look like two sections.
Each card is white inside with a thin accent border and rounded corners; the numbered circle sits
on the card's top edge, half in and half out, with a white numeral. Title first, the two
instruction paragraphs under it, and the photo filling the lower half to two-thirds of the card.

A small accent arrow between one card and the next, vertically centred, not touching either card.

The row of cards is the dominant content and should fill roughly three quarters of the square's
height. If the text will not stay readable, make the photos smaller — never drop to four cards.

FOOTER: one full-width solid strip in the accent colour across the very bottom, holding FOUR
items separated by thin vertical dividers. Each is a simple white line icon with two lines of
white capitals beside it, e.g.

  COMPLETE DECORATION KIT | EASY TO SET UP IN MINUTES | PERFECT FOR MEMORABLE PHOTOS |
  IDEAL FOR <the occasion>

Only phrases that are true of THIS pack. Keep the strip thin — it is a finishing band, not a
second content block, and it never grows into rows of product cards.

=== TEXT ===

Spell every word correctly and read the whole image back word by word before you finish. Product
wording is reproduced exactly as the pack writes it — "GROOM TO BE" stays "GROOM TO BE".

The only text in the image is: the header, the pill, the five titles, the five pairs of
instruction sentences, and the four footer phrases. No slogans, no extra decorative text.

Never use a quality or urgency claim anywhere: premium, luxury, royal, best, high quality,
100% original, superior, finest, exclusive, best seller, trending, limited stock. Say what the
kit contains or what the buyer can do with it — nothing about how good it is.

From IMAGE 2 take shape, colour, finish, wording and appearance only. Never copy a seller tag,
a Meesho code, a watermark, a logo, a price, a border, a badge or any printed text off it.
