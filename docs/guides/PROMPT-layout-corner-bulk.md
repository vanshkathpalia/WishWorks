That photograph is good. Keep it. Regenerate it with ONE change: the balloons now BUNCH UP INTO
THE TOP CORNERS in thick, full clusters, and thin out everywhere else.

THE TOTAL NUMBER OF BALLOONS DOES NOT CHANGE. This is a move, not an addition. Every balloon that
goes into a corner comes OUT of the run along the top and the sides — one in, one out, and the one
you remove is the SAME COLOUR and the SAME SIZE as the one you added, so the colour mix and the
per-item counts still match the list exactly. Do not add a single balloon. Do not duplicate one.
Do not shrink them to fit more in: a balloon stays the size it is, and the counts are a cap.

WHAT A BULKY CORNER LOOKS LIKE, so it reads as full rather than crowded:
- Balloons packed tight against the corner with no wall showing between them, overlapping in two
  or three layers of depth so the cluster looks deep and not flat.
- The biggest balloons on the outside of the cluster, the smallest tucked into the gaps.
- The cluster is DENSEST right in the corner and tapers as it runs away from it, so the eye reads
  a heavy corner rather than a thick stripe.
- A few balloons may sit right against the top edge and be cropped by it. That is normal and looks
  correct.

THE REST OF THE PICTURE GETS SPARSER, AND THAT IS RIGHT. The garland between the corners becomes a
thin, well-spaced line — single balloons with wall visible between them, or a run that fades out
entirely. A sparse middle with heavy corners is the look I am asking for. A picture that kept the
middle full AND added heavy corners has used more balloons than I sell, which is the one thing
that must not happen.

The banner, the foil letters and anything with writing on it stay exactly where they are, at the
same size, fully visible. A corner cluster must not cover, crop or crowd them.

Everything else is identical — the same items, the same exact counts from the list, the same plain
wall and daylight, the same square 1:1 shape, still nothing in the scene that is not on the list,
still zero text anywhere in the image.
